mTiny

Connecting…

Shows

Moves, eyes and sound together. Give the robot about half a metre of clear floor.

Ours

Built into the robot

Built-in moves are replayed step by step over Bluetooth, so quick wiggles are a little slower than the original. Add your own shows in sequences.json.

Drive

Each press drives for the step time, then stops. Arrow keys work too; space stops.

Eyes

Tap a face to show it.

Sounds

Tap a sound to play it. New clips must first be copied to mTiny by USB.
Advanced

Single wheel pulse

Left and right wheel power, positive is forward. Capped at ±200 and 250 ms.
